# General Project Flow

## Overview

This document describes the standard project lifecycle with six distinct phases, governance gates, and decision criteria. Each phase has defined deliverables and Go/no-Go gates that determine progression to the next phase.

---

## Phase 1: Idea

**Purpose:** Establish and assess the initial project concept

### Key Activities & Deliverables

- Define idea rationale: problem, opportunity, or need
- Establish objective and intended outcome
- Provide initial cost and benefit estimates
- Identify involved stakeholders and decision-makers
- Assess alignment with strategic objectives (demonstrable contribution)

### Governance Gate: Project Idea Approval

**Decision Maker:** Portfolio Board Preparation

**Go/no-Go Criteria:**
- Project Idea Approval

**Progression:** Approved ideas move to the Definition phase

---

## Phase 2: Definition

**Purpose:** Develop detailed project structure and planning

### Key Activities & Deliverables

- Establish project governance structure
- Conduct project kickoff with all stakeholders, including sponsor and project manager
- Develop detailed project scope and requirements
- Refine budget and resource estimates
- Document project approach and methodology

### Governance Gate: Project Concept Approval

**Decision Maker:** Portfolio Board Preparation

**Go/no-Go Criteria:**
- Goedkeuring Concept (Concept Approval)
- Project Brief approval

**Progression:** Approved concepts proceed to Design phase

---

## Phase 3: Design

**Purpose:** Detail the solution architecture and implementation approach

### Key Activities & Deliverables

- Develop detailed requirements and specifications
- Identify and engage content/subject matter experts
- Coordinate technical and operational requirements
- Refine detailed budget and resource planning
- Conduct design reviews and validations

### Governance Gate: Project Initiation Document (PID) Approval

**Decision Maker:** Portfolio Board Preparation

**Go/no-Go Criteria:**
- Goedkeuring Definitieve Project Brief / Project Initiation Document (PID) Approval
- Optional: Project Start Architecture V1.0 Review

**Progression:** Approved projects move to Realization phase

---

## Phase 4: Realization

**Purpose:** Execute the project and deliver the solution

### Key Activities & Deliverables

- Execute planned work according to project schedule
- Manage project execution and coordination
- Select, assess and direct external partners/suppliers
- Monitor status and manage changes and deviations
- Manage identified risks and issues
- Maintain project log documenting decisions, risks, issues, changes and actions
- Conduct bi-weekly steering group meetings

### Governance Gate: Status Reporting & Escalation Management

**Decision Maker:** Steering Group

**Go/no-Go Criteria:**
- Status Report with requested decisions and escalations
- Optional: Project Start Architecture V1.0 updates

**Progression:** Upon completion of all realization activities, move to Go-Live phase

---

## Phase 5: Go-Live (Livegang)

**Purpose:** Prepare and execute the transition to operational status

### Key Activities & Deliverables

- Execute comprehensive testing of requirements and acceptance criteria
- Establish support organization and procedures
- Assign responsibility for operations and continuity management
- Conduct end-user training and knowledge transfer
- Finalize operational readiness

### Governance Gate: Go-Live Approval

**Decision Maker:** Steering Group

**Go/no-Go Criteria:**
- Goedgekeurd verzoek tot livegang (Approved Go-Live Request)
- Steering Group approval required for proceeding

**Progression:** Upon approval, system goes live; transition to Handover and Closure phase

---

## Phase 6: Handover and Closure

**Purpose:** Complete project, transfer to operations, and document lessons learned

### Key Activities & Deliverables

- Perform final validation of all requirements and acceptance criteria
- Execute full go-live and operational commencement
- Provide post-implementation support (Nazorg)
- Complete knowledge transfer and documentation
- Conduct project closure review and lessons learned
- Transition all support responsibilities to operations team

### Governance Gate: Project Documentation & Closure Approval

**Decision Maker:** Portfolio Board Preparation

**Go/no-Go Criteria:**
- Project Documentation Complete
- Project Closure Approval

**Progression:** Project formally closed; lessons incorporated into organizational knowledge

---

## Governance Structure

### Portfolio Board

**Role:** Strategic oversight and major decision authority
- Approves progression at Idea, Definition, and Design phases
- Reviews project alignment with strategic objectives
- Receives escalations from Steering Group when needed

### Steering Group

**Role:** Operational oversight and tactical management during Realization and Go-Live
- Meets bi-weekly during Realization phase
- Receives status reports and manages escalations
- Approves critical changes and risk responses
- Approves go-live readiness and transition

---

## Key Principles

1. **Clear Gate Decisions:** Each phase has defined Go/no-Go criteria; projects must meet criteria to progress

2. **Iterative Refinement:** Information becomes more detailed and concrete with each phase

3. **Risk Management:** Risks identified and managed continuously; escalations managed through appropriate governance

4. **Stakeholder Involvement:** Key stakeholders engaged throughout project lifecycle

5. **Decision Quality:** Governance structure ensures informed decision-making with appropriate authority levels

6. **Documentation:** All decisions, risks, issues, changes and actions are documented in project logs

---

## Mapping to Advisory Assessments

| Phase | Advisory Role | Purpose |
|-------|---------------|---------|
| Idea | Project Idea Advisor | Assess early concept clarity and strategic fit |
| Definition | Project Brief Advisor | Review detailed brief before Realization approval |
| Design | PID Advisor, PSA Advisor | Evaluate Project Initiation Document and architecture |
| Realization | Project Execution | Manage delivery under Steering Group oversight |
| Go-Live | Project Execution | Manage transition under Steering Group approval |
| Closure | Project Closure Advisor | Assess completion and approve closure |
