# Decision Authority Matrix

*Reference for who makes which decisions at each gate.*

| Gate | Decision Required | Approval Criteria | Decision Authority | Timeline |
|------|-------------------|----|---|---|
| **IDEA** | Project Idea Approval | Strategic fit, Business case soundness, Resource feasibility | Portfolio Board | Within 2 weeks |
| **DEFINITION** | Project Brief Approval | Scope clarity, Feasibility, Business value, Risk acceptable | Portfolio Board | Within 2 weeks |
| **DESIGN** | PID & Architecture Approval | Technical soundness, Budget realistic, Timeline feasible, Steering Group structure ready | Portfolio Board + Steering Group | Within 2 weeks |
| **REALIZATION** | Status & Escalation Management | Project progress vs. plan, Issues resolved or escalated, Risks managed | Steering Group (bi-weekly) | Bi-weekly |
| **GO-LIVE** | Go-Live Approval | Deliverables accepted, Quality acceptable, Operational readiness, Training complete | Steering Group + Project Sponsor | Within 1 week |
| **CLOSURE** | Project Closure Approval | Deliverables accepted, Benefits validated, Lessons learned documented, Issues handed over | Portfolio Board + Project Sponsor | Within 2 weeks |
